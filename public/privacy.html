Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Altitude Films ("we") runs this website and an internal tool, Altitude Films Tools, that publishes our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

Website

The booking form does not store data on our servers. When you press a send button, your message opens in WhatsApp or your email app, and you send it yourself. We only use what you send us to answer you and plan your shoot.

Altitude Films Tools uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos on our own channel only. It does not access other users’ data. Its use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
